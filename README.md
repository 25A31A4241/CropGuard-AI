\# 🌱 CropGuard AI



AI-powered crop disease detection and intelligent crop health monitoring system.



\## 🎯 Problem



Farmers often identify crop diseases only after visible symptoms become serious.

This can result in crop loss, unnecessary pesticide usage, and reduced income.



\## 💡 Our Solution



CropGuard AI uses deep learning to analyze crop leaf images and identify possible diseases or healthy conditions.



Our long-term vision is to combine disease detection with weather, environmental conditions, location, and risk analysis to provide useful recommendations to farmers.



\## 🧠 Current AI Prototype



\- Model: ResNet18 with Transfer Learning

\- Framework: PyTorch

\- Image Size: 224 × 224

\- Crops Supported: Apple, Grape, Potato, Tomato

\- Classes: 21

\- Training Images: 22,036

\- Validation Images: 2,754

\- Validation Accuracy: 98.69%
